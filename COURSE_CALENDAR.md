# 11-Week Course Calendar

| Week | Lecture (3 h) | Hands-on (4 h) | Assessment milestone |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | Statistical thinking, probability, generative view | Catalogue anatomy and robust summaries | Start HW1 |
| 2 | Hypothesis testing, bootstrap, permutation, FDR | Mechanism-group comparisons | Submit HW1 |
| 3 | Optimisation, likelihood, MLE | Population likelihoods and misspecification | Start HW2 |
| 4 | Bayesian inference and posterior prediction | Priors, posteriors, predictive checks | Continue HW2 |
| 5 | MCMC and hierarchical inference | Sampling diagnostics + hierarchical formulation | Submit HW2 |
| 6 | Selection effects and survey inference | Intrinsic vs detected population | HW3 |
| 7 | Irregular time series, red noise, GP | Real simulated light curves | HW4 |
| 8 | Machine learning for inference | Baseline + flexible predictive model | Start HW5 |
| 9 | Trustworthy ML and domain shift | Leakage audit, calibration, OOD | Submit HW5 |
| 10 | Deep probabilistic models and SBI | Mini-SBI + calibration | HW6 |
| 11 | Active follow-up, Bayesian optimisation, scaling | Final-project launch / analysis clinic | Final project + presentation |

The instructor may shift homework deadlines by one week depending on local semester regulations.
